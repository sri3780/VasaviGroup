Classes 6th to 10th

Integrated IIT & NEET Foundation Program

Designed for high school students aspiring for Engineering (IIT-JEE) and Medical (NEET) careers. Our curriculum strengthens core Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Biology concepts with analytical problem-solving skills.

Concept-Oriented Syllabus: Deep-dive into fundamental concepts in Mathematics & Sciences beyond regular SSC textbooks.
Speed & Accuracy Training: Analytical reasoning, mental math shortcuts, and weekly MCQ objective tests.
Expert STEM Mentors: Guidance from experienced faculty specialized in IIT & NEET foundation coaching.
Classes 5th & 6th

Navodaya & Sainik School Entrance Coaching

Specialized entrance exam preparation for 5th and 6th-grade students aiming for admissions into Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas (JNVST) and Sainik Schools (AISSEE) across Telangana and India.

Proven Results (2/10 Selected): Last year 2 students out of 10 applicants secured Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya seats.
Mental Ability & Logic: Intensive training in spatial reasoning, pattern matching, visual logic, and diagrammatic puzzles.
Mock Exams & OMR Practice: Regular full-length practice tests on official OMR sheets to build time management.
